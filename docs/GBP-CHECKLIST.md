The profile already has customer photos. Owner photos rank and convert better because they are current and well-lit.

Upload at minimum:

- **Cover** — the storefront with the lit sign, shot at dusk
- **Exterior** — 3 shots: street view, the sign, the entrance with the State Police HQ visible as a landmark
- **Interior** — 5 shots: the full display case, the cake case, the aisles, the counter, the room at 7 AM with fresh bread out
- **Product** — 15+ shots, individually: tres leches, quesadilla, conchas, flan, churros, hojaldres, donuts, and 4 decorated cakes

Then upload **2–3 new photos every week**, permanently. Profiles with recent photos outrank identical profiles without them.

Every photo: 720×720 minimum, JPG or PNG, well-lit, warm-graded, no filters, no text overlays, no borders.

## Phase 4 — Reviews

**663 reviews and not one owner response.** That is the single biggest missed signal on the profile.

### The backlog
Respond to the most recent 30 reviews. Then work backwards as time allows.

### The 1-star from ~April 2026
It says the pastries were "the driest and most bland." Respond publicly, once, warmly, without arguing:

> Gracias por decírnoslo, y lamentamos mucho que no estuviera fresco. Horneamos todo cada mañana y eso no debió pasar. Nos gustaría arreglarlo — por favor llámanos al (804) 986-9695 y pregunta por la familia.

Then fix the underlying thing. Freshness is this bakery's stated vulnerability, and the website's answer to it — *baked by hand every morning, conchas out at 7* — has to be true every day.

### Ongoing cadence
- Respond to **every** review within 48 hours.
- Reply in the language the review was written in.
- Vary the wording. Ten identical "Thank you for your review!" replies read as a bot and Google notices.
- Never argue. Never mention a competitor. Never offer a discount to remove a review — that violates Google's policy.

### Getting more
The `review-request` email from `PROMPT-14` sends 24 hours after a completed order, once per customer per 60 days, linking straight to the Google review form.

**Never gate the request on satisfaction.** Asking "were you happy?" and only showing the Google link to the ones who say yes is review gating — it violates Google's policy and it is dishonest.

## Phase 5 — Posts and Q&A

### Posts — weekly, permanently

Google Posts expire after 7 days. A profile posting weekly outranks an identical profile that never posts.

Rotation:

| Week | Post |
|---|---|
| 1 | What's fresh — *"Las conchas salen a las 7"* with a photo |
| 2 | A cake — a real quinceañera or wedding cake from that month |
| 3 | Seasonal — rosca de reyes (January), pan de muerto (October), Semana Santa, Mother's Day / Día de las Madres |
| 4 | The wholesale pitch — *"¿Tienes una tienda?"* |

Every post: one photo, two or three sentences in Spanish and English, and a CTA button linking to the right page.

### Q&A — seed it

Owners may post and answer their own questions, and most never do. Seed these eight:

1. ¿A qué hora sale el pan fresco? / What time is fresh bread out?
2. ¿Hacen pasteles de quinceañera? / Do you make quinceañera cakes?
3. ¿Con cuánta anticipación debo pedir un pastel? / How far ahead for a cake?
4. ¿Tienen quesadilla salvadoreña? / Do you have Salvadoran cheese bread?
5. ¿Venden al por mayor a tiendas? / Do you sell wholesale to stores?
6. ¿Hay estacionamiento? / Is there parking? *(client-confirmed)*
7. ¿Aceptan tarjeta? / Do you take cards?
8. ¿Se puede pedir en línea? / Can I order online?

Answer each in both languages, factually, with the phone number where relevant.
