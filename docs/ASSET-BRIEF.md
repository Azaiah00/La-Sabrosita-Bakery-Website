# Asset Brief — La Sabrosita Bakery

Two parts: **the photography the client must shoot** (everything a customer will order), and **the AI-generated plates we produce** (atmosphere, texture, motion backgrounds only).

> **The rule that governs this whole document:** real photographs of the real product, always. AI imagery is for atmosphere, texture, backgrounds and motion plates — **never for a dish a customer will order**, and never in a way that misrepresents portion, plating or ingredients. A guest who orders a concha because of a generated image and receives something different has been misled, and that is not a design decision we get to make.
>
> There is currently **not one photograph of one product** on their entire website. This brief is the single highest-return line item in the engagement.

---

## Part 1 — The shoot (client's real product)

### Logistics

**When:** two sessions. Session A at **6:30–8:30 AM** on a weekday, when the case is full and the morning light is coming through the front windows. Session B at **2:00–3:30 PM** for the cakes, when the decorators are working and the room is quiet.

**Who:** a local food photographer. Budget $800–1,500 for both sessions in the Richmond market. This is not the place to save money — these images carry the entire site.

**Kit:** full-frame body; 50mm and 100mm macro for product; 35mm for the room; one large diffused light source plus a reflector. Shoot tethered so the client can approve on the spot.

**Grading:** warm. Amber, caramel, deep char, honey. **No cool blue-white** — it kills appetite response. Push the whites slightly warm, keep the shadows soft and brown, never crushed to grey.

**Styling:** minimal and honest. Real trays, real paper, real hands. Crumbs stay. No plastic props, no fake steam, no oversized garnish that does not come with the item. If the concha has a crack in the sugar shell, that crack is the photograph.

### Video — one vertical loop

10–15 seconds, 9:16, no audio needed. Options in order of preference: bread coming out of the oven; the sugar shell being scored; the milk being poured over a tres leches. Shot at 60fps for a half-speed slow-motion option. Deliver as MP4 (H.264) **under 2.5 MB after compression**, plus a poster frame.

### Delivery

Full-resolution originals plus web exports at 2400px, 1600px, 800px and 400px on the long edge. AVIF and WebP. Named descriptively — `concha-macro-shell-01.jpg`, never `IMG_4471.jpg`.

**Every file needs bilingual alt text written by whoever was on the shoot**, because they know what is actually in the frame. Not "cake" — *"Pastel de tres leches de dos pisos con listones color vino"*.

## Part 2 — AI-generated plates (Higgsfield)

**Permitted uses only:** background texture, atmosphere plates behind copy, section dividers, motion loops that contain no identifiable product, and social ad creative that does not depict a specific orderable item.

**Never:** a dish, a cake, a pastry, the storefront, the room, or any person associated with the business.

### Model selection

| Job | Model |
|---|---|
| Stills, texture, background plates | Nano Banana / Flux / Seedream |
| Motion loops | Veo / Kling / Seedance |
| Social ad creative | Marketing Studio |

Every prompt specifies subject, lens and focal length, lighting, color grade, motion and duration.

### Plate 1 — Flour dust, hero background texture

> Extreme macro of fine wheat flour dust suspended in a shaft of warm morning light against a deep near-black background. 100mm macro lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field. Single hard key light from the upper left creating visible light rays through the particles. Color grade: warm amber highlights, deep brown-black shadows, no blue in the shadows. Absolutely no objects, no hands, no food, no text. Fine-grain film texture. 16:9.

Use: behind the hero copy at 12% opacity, over `#16130F`.

### Plate 2 — Warm paper texture, chamber background

> Seamless macro texture of aged cream-colored bakery parchment paper, subtle fiber and grain visible, very slight warm discoloration. Flat even diffuse lighting, no shadows, no objects. Color grade: warm bone, hex approximately #FDFBF6 with soft #E4D8C4 fiber variation. Tileable. No text, no logos, no folds, no creases. 1:1.

Use: at 4% opacity over the paper chambers, for tactile depth.

### Plate 3 — Wheat field bokeh, wholesale band

> Out-of-focus golden wheat stalks backlit by low late-afternoon sun, extremely shallow depth of field so nothing is identifiable, pure warm bokeh. 200mm lens, f/2.0. Color grade: honey, amber, wheat — approximately #E2AA67 dominant. No sky, no horizon, no buildings, no people. 21:9.

Use: behind the wholesale chamber at low opacity.

### Plate 4 — Oven heat shimmer, motion loop

> Seamless 6-second loop of heat shimmer rising against a dark charred surface. Subtle refractive distortion only, no visible flame, no smoke, no objects. Slow upward drift. Camera locked off. Color grade: deep char with faint ember-orange warmth at the base, approximately #D16639. No food, no oven, no hands visible. 1080×1920 vertical, seamless loop.

Use: hero motion layer, `preload="none"`, skipped under reduced motion.

### Plate 5 — Sugar crystals macro, divider texture

> Extreme macro of coarse sugar crystals catching warm directional light, individual crystals refracting, dark background falling to black. 100mm macro, f/4. Warm golden highlights, no cool tones. No food, no hands, no text. 16:9.

Use: chamber divider band.

### Social ad creative — Marketing Studio

Permitted for awareness ads that do **not** depict a specific orderable product: abstract warm texture with typography, seasonal announcements, wholesale B2B creative. Any ad showing an actual pastry uses a real photograph from Part 1.

## Part 3 — The logo

Current state: **raster only**. No SVG, no vector source. The wordmark is a stock geometric bold and will go soft at any size above a phone.

Sampled values, measured pixel-by-pixel, not estimated:

| Element | Hex |
|---|---|
| Banner background | `#E2AA67` |
| Circle mark fill | `#D16639` |
| Wordmark ink | `#191918` |
| Toque | `#FFFFFF` |

### The work

1. **Trace the circular baker mark as clean SVG.** Three separate paths — ring, toque, face — so it can draw itself. This is the signature motion for the entire site. Optimize under 4 KB. 2–3 hours.
2. **Re-set the wordmark in EB Garamond** at the same size relationship, same layout, same colors. It will read as *panadería* instead of *print shop*, and the customer will perceive it as the same logo in focus — not a new one.
3. **Replace the tagline.** "Pastries, Specialty Cakes and much more." says nothing; *"and much more"* is the phrase a business uses when it has not decided what it is. Replace with the positioning line, bilingual.
4. **Deliver:** SVG (mark alone, horizontal lockup, stacked lockup), PNG at 4 sizes, favicon set, a 512×512 PWA icon, and a 720×720 square for the Google Business Profile.

**Do not rebrand.** 663 reviews and a lit sign on Midlothian Turnpike are equity. Sharpen it; do not throw it away.
